About me

Data Scientist

I hold a master’s degree in Astrophysics and a background in Applied Mathematics. My training in mathematics and scientific research has shaped a problem-solving approach grounded in analysis, evidence, and critical thinking.

I work with Python, SQL, and machine learning to develop predictive models and analyze data. Through the TripleTen Data Science program, I completed projects in classification, regression, time series forecasting, natural language processing, and computer vision.

In the MEGASTAR project, I worked on scientific data processing, analysis, and automation, developing tools that reduced processing time by approximately 95%. I am currently seeking opportunities to grow as a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer.
